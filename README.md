### ⭐ Featured project: Tool Crib Warehouse Implementation (SYSPRO)
*Owner, end-to-end · precision manufacturing*

Turned an honor-system tool crib into a controlled, trackable SYSPRO warehouse.

- **Audited 2,087 SKUs** and found that 46% had no real bin location, 49% showed zero on-hand, 57% had no min/max levels and 96% were missing pricing. Used that data to scope and prioritize cleanup.
- **Wrote the SOPs** for item issuance, authorization, min/max replenishment and non-moving inventory disposition.
- **Kept accountability during the transition** with a temporary manual sign-out process, and trained 1st and 2nd shift teams on the new controls.
- **Sourced and negotiated physical security upgrades** (access control and cameras), securing better pricing and moving installation **more than two weeks earlier**.
- Owned it from process design through vendor negotiation, installation and staff communication.
